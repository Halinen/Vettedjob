## Work eligibility

If a posting explicitly requires citizenship, an active security clearance, or
work authorization that is not stated in the candidate profile, keep `visa_ok`
conservative and explain the risk in `visa_note`. Do not reject a role solely
because sponsorship is not mentioned.

## Scoring preferences

- Score 8-10 only when the posting clearly matches one of the two target
  directions: systems-oriented ML/AI software engineering, or quant dev/research
  internship.
- Score 8-10 only if the role is based in Toronto / Greater Toronto Area, or is
  explicitly remote while open to Toronto-based candidates.
- Score 6-7 when the role is adjacent but still plausibly useful, such as backend
  C++ infrastructure with some ML/performance component, or quant-adjacent data
  engineering with strong Python/C++/statistics requirements.
- Score below 6 for generic software/data roles without C++, ML/AI systems,
  performance optimization, or quant relevance.
- Score below 6 for postings that are clearly US-only, outside Canada, or not
  open to Toronto/GTA candidates.
- For quant searches, internships/co-ops should be favored over senior full-time
  roles.
